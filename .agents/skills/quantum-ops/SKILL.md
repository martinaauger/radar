# Quantum Radar — Operativa

## Scope

This skill is for **Martina’s operativa view**, not the published consultant directory.

Do:
- Work queues: solo LinkedIn, pending mail, sin canal, potenciales socios
- Enrich contact channels (mail + LinkedIn)
- Mark / unmark `asociado` (potencial socio)
- Fill `cvSummary` (dónde trabajó, qué hace, país)
- Import CSV and attribute owners (Fernando, Elías, César, Laura)
- Keep two histories separate:
  - **Antecedentes Quantum · SGQ** = trabajos de Quantum con la *empresa* (EPEC, etc.). Data comes from SGQ; never invent.
  - **Actividad en el Radar** = cómo entró el contacto al directorio (Laura, LinkedIn, tags). Useful, but not SGQ work history.

Do not:
- Put work queues or enrichment UI on the consultant Resumen
- Highlight “regulación” as a vanity KPI
- Call Radar import/tag events “Historial Quantum” or invent SGQ project work

## Contact model reminders

- Lists of interesados + export/campaigns = future user feature (not this skill yet)
